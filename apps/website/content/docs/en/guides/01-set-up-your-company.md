# Set up your company

This guide takes you from an empty company to a ship your office can sell, in the order Riverdesk expects. You do it once when the company opens, and again for each new ship. Keep your cabin plan, route and price list at hand.

## Before you start

- You can sign in as an **Owner**. Only owners can set up ships, schedules and rates. See [Signing in for the first time](/docs/getting-started/first-sign-in).
- The ship's cabin list: numbers, decks and categories.
- The weekly route (ports and days) and the cruises you sell on it, such as 4 nights from Luxor.
- Your seasons, prices, and cancellation, child and payment terms.

Each step builds on the one before, so follow the order.

## Ships & cabins

Open **Ships & cabins**.

1. **Press + Add ship and describe the ship.** Type the **Ship name** as guests and agencies know it (for example _MS Nile Pearl_), a short **Code** in capitals (_PEARL_), the **Type** and **Guests on board (maximum)**, the ship's licensed limit. The code cannot change later.

   ![Adding a ship](/screens/{locale}/guide-add-ship.jpg 'The Add ship dialog filled in for MS Nile Pearl, a cruise ship for 64 guests')

2. **Press + Add category for each kind of cabin you sell.** A category is a kind of cabin sold at one price, such as Deluxe River View. Give it names in English and Arabic (shown on vouchers), a code such as `DLX`, and the **Adults (maximum)** and **Guests in total (maximum)**: 2 and 3 means two adults and one child.
3. **Add the optional details.** Pick the **Deck**, **View** and **Beds** from the lists, or choose **Other…** to type your own. Tick **Extra bed possible** where it applies.

4. **Press + Add cabins and type the cabin numbers.** Choose the category and type the numbers. Ranges work: `201-212` adds twelve cabins, and `101-110, 201, 202` mixes ranges and single numbers. Riverdesk shows how many cabins will be added before you confirm.
5. **Finish the cabin details and confirm.** Add the deck, tick **Wheelchair accessible** where it applies, and pick a reason such as _Initial setup_. Leave **From departures on** at today's date for a new ship.

   ![Adding cabins](/screens/{locale}/guide-add-cabins.jpg 'Twelve cabins, 201 to 212, added to one category in a single step')

> [!TIP]
> If a number is already on the ship, nothing is added and Riverdesk lists the duplicates, so you never end up with half a batch.

## Schedule

Open **Schedule** and choose the ship at the top. You describe where the ship sails; Riverdesk turns it into departures.

1. **Add the ports.** In the **Ports** card, press **Add the usual Nile ports**. Nine ports, from Luxor to Abu Simbel and Cairo, are added in one click. Use **+ Add port** for any other. Ports are shared by all your ships.
2. **Press + Add route and set the cycle.** A route is the ship's repeating cycle of stops. Name it (_Luxor ⇄ Aswan weekly_), set **Repeats every (days)** to 7, and pick the **First departure**: its weekday becomes the departure day.
3. **Add the stops in order.** Day 0 is the first port. Press **+ Add stop** for each port with its day, for example Luxor day 0, Edfu day 2, Aswan day 4. Riverdesk fills in the last stop, Luxor on day 7, where the next cycle starts.
4. **Tick Board / leave where guests can start or end a cruise.** Usually that is Luxor and Aswan. Riverdesk then shows what **Each cycle sails**, for example "Luxor → Aswan · 4 nights, Aswan → Luxor · 3 nights".

   ![Adding a route](/screens/{locale}/guide-route.jpg 'A weekly Luxor ⇄ Aswan route with its stops, days and boarding points')

> [!WARNING]
> Once a route has itineraries or departures, its stops are fixed, so that what you have sold never changes underneath. To sail a different route later, give the old one an end date and add a new one.

5. **Press + Add itinerary for each cruise you sell.** Choose **Board at** and **Leave at**. Riverdesk counts the nights and suggests a code and names, such as `LXR-4` and "4 nights from Luxor". A stop in the **next cycle** makes a longer cruise, such as 7 nights round trip.

6. **Press Extend schedule at the bottom of the departures.** Riverdesk creates 18 months of departures and sailings and tells you how many. It never changes existing departures, so it is safe to press again.

## Rates & policies

Open **Rates & policies** and choose the same ship. Add seasons, then policies, then a rate plan, then prices: each needs the one before.

1. **Press + Add season for each season.** Give it a code (`HIGH`, fixed later), a name and one or more date ranges, for example High from 1 October to 30 April. A date belongs to one season only.

> [!NOTE]
> A sailing is priced with the season of its **embarkation date**, for all its nights. A 4-night cruise that leaves Luxor on 28 April is priced entirely at High season, even though it ends in May. Sailings no season covers are listed in a yellow note and cannot be sold.

2. **Add a cancellation policy.** In the **Cancellation** card, press **+ Add**. Each step keeps a share of the booking from a number of days before embarkation: 0% from 60 days, 25% from 30, 100% from 0. The 0-day step is required.
3. **Add a child policy.** In the **Children** card, press **+ Add**. Set **Adult from age** (usually 12), the **Children per cabin (maximum)**, and the **Age bands**: for example under 2 free, 2 to 5 at 25% and 6 to 11 at 50% of the adult price.
4. **Add a payment policy.** In the **Payment** card, press **+ Add**. Set the **Deposit %** taken at confirmation (25%, say), the **Balance due (days before embarkation)** (30), and how long cabins wait for a transfer or card payment.

Policies are shared by all your ships. Bookings keep the policy they were sold with; a change applies to new quotes only.

5. **Press + New rate plan and describe the price list.** A rate plan is a price list for one market and currency, such as _International (USD)_. Fill in the name and code, **Market**, **Currency**, **Price per** (person or cabin), **Price for** (each night or the whole itinerary), **Board**, **Minimum nights** and the three policies.
6. **List what the price includes.** Type **Included** and **Not included** one item per line, in both languages. They print on vouchers.
7. **Leave Sell in the back office ticked, and save.** This creates the back-office contract for the ship, so your staff can book the plan at once from **New booking**. Agencies get their own contracts later, on [Channels](/docs/staff-guide/channels).

   ![A new rate plan](/screens/{locale}/guide-plan.jpg 'The New rate plan dialog, with its market, currency, price basis and policies')

8. **Type the prices.** Choose the **Rate plan** and the **Season** (and the **Itinerary** for a plan priced for the whole itinerary). Type a row per category; per-person plans have single, double and triple columns, for example USD 620 per person sharing a Deluxe River View. Press **Save rates**.
9. **Repeat for every season.** A sailing sells only when its category has a price in its season.

   ![Typing prices](/screens/{locale}/guide-prices.jpg 'The price grid for one plan and season: one row per category, one column per occupancy')

> [!WARNING]
> Once a plan has prices, its market, currency and the way prices are counted are fixed, because the prices would mean something else. If you chose the wrong currency, make a new plan.

## Check Getting started on Today

Open **Today**. Until a ship can be sold, owners see **Getting started** at the top: the ten setup steps for each ship, ticked when done. The next step is highlighted, and each links to its screen.

![Getting started](/screens/{locale}/guide-getting-started.jpg 'Getting started on Today, with the ten steps for one ship and the next step highlighted')

Follow the link of any unticked step; common gaps are sailings with no season and missing prices. When all are ticked, the list disappears.

## Invite the team and set the operations email

Open **Settings**.

1. **Invite each colleague under People.** Press **Invite someone**, type their email and name, and choose their role: **Reservations**, **Finance** or **Read only**. They sign in with that email to join. See [People and roles](/docs/getting-started/people-and-roles).
2. **Set the Operations email (nightly outage pack).** Use a shared office inbox, not one person's address. It receives the nightly pack of the next 14 days, for when the internet fails.
3. **Fill in the voucher details and press Save.** Under **On every voucher**, add the **Emergency phone (on vouchers)** and the voucher note in English and Arabic: boarding point and check-in time.

## Markets (optional)

Riverdesk starts with International and Egyptian resident. For another price list, such as Gulf in SAR, press **+ Add market** in **Settings**, with names in both languages and a lower-case code (`gcc`). Tick **Guests must show proof of residency or ID at boarding** for resident prices.

Then make a rate plan in that market.

## What happens next

Your ship is ready to sell from **New booking**. Next, set up your agencies on **Channels**, and import existing bookings if you are moving from another system.
