# Answer an agency request

Some agencies sell your cabins **on request**: they ask for a sailing, and you decide. This guide shows how to read a request, give one of the three answers, and what the agency sees afterwards. You will do this several times a day in high season, and each request has a deadline.

## Before you start

- You can sign in as **Reservations** or **Owner**.
- The agency has an on-request contract on **Channels**. See [Connect a travel agency](/docs/guides/connect-a-travel-agency).

A request takes no cabins until you accept it. That is the point of on-request selling: you keep control of who gets your last cabins, but the agency is waiting, so answer quickly.

## Read the request

1. **Open Requests.** You can also follow **Requests waiting for an answer** on **Today**, which shows the hours left for each. The nearest deadline comes first.
2. **Check what is asked.** Each request shows its reference and the agency, the embarkation date, the itinerary and nights, each cabin with its category and guests (`DLX 2+6` means two adults and a six-year-old), and the price the agency's contract gives.
3. **Look at the time left.** The badge shows the time left before the deadline. It turns red in the last two hours, and reads **Past the deadline** once the deadline has gone.

   ![The Requests screen](/screens/{locale}/guide-requests.jpg 'Open requests, nearest deadline first, with the time left on each')

> [!NOTE]
> The deadline is the time in the agency's contract (6 hours unless you agreed otherwise) plus the grace period in **Settings** (2 hours by default). If nobody answers within that, the request **expires** and the agency is told. Response times show on the Pilot dashboard, so try never to let one lapse.

If you need more detail before deciding, open the reference: it leads to the booking page with the guests and the full price.

## Give your answer

Choose one of the three answers. Each one is final for this request, so check the cabins on **Inventory** first if you are unsure.

### Accept

1. **Press Accept.** Riverdesk takes the cabins and confirms the booking in the same step, and shows _Accepted_ with the reference. For an agency that pays you directly, the booking is held instead while the agency pays, as for any prepaid booking.
2. **If the cabins have gone in the meantime,** Riverdesk says _Not enough cabins left for this selection._ and nothing changes. Offer another sailing instead.

### Offer another sailing

1. **Press Offer another sailing.** Riverdesk lists sailings of the same ship from three weeks before to six weeks after the requested date that have room for the same cabins.
2. **Choose a sailing and press Send offer.** The same cabins and guests are offered on that date, at the price the agency's contract gives for it. The request then shows _Alternative offered_ with the date and when the offer expires.

   ![Offering another sailing](/screens/{locale}/guide-request-offer.jpg 'The Offer another sailing dialog, listing nearby dates with room for the same cabins')

While the offer is open, its cabins are held for the agency, so nobody else can sell them. The agency has 48 hours to accept, the **Alternative offer valid (hours)** in **Settings**. If it accepts, the booking moves to the new sailing. If it does nothing, the offer expires and the cabins return to sale.

> [!TIP]
> Before offering, check with the agency by phone if you can. An offer it was expecting is accepted quickly; a surprise offer often sits until it expires, holding cabins you could have sold.

### Decline

1. **Press Decline.** A dialog asks for the reason.
2. **Choose a Reason (sent to the agency) and press Decline.** Pick one from the list, such as _No cabins left in that category_ or _Dates not available_, or type your own. Write it for the agency: they read it word for word.

## What the agency sees

The agency is told of your answer straight away. An agency connected to Riverdesk through its own system receives the decision there: accepted, declined with your reason, an alternative offered with its expiry, or timed out. If your own staff entered the request on the agency's behalf from **New booking**, call or email the agency with the answer.

| Your answer           | The request becomes          | The agency                                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Accept                | Confirmed (or held, prepaid) | Has a booking and can send its guest a voucher |
| Offer another sailing | Alternative offered          | Accepts within 48 hours, or lets it lapse      |
| Decline               | Declined                     | Sees your reason                               |
| No answer in time     | Expired                      | Is told it was not answered                    |

## What happens next

An accepted request is an ordinary booking from now on: it appears on **Bookings**, and you complete the guests, print documents and handle changes there. A declined or expired request takes nothing, so there is nothing to clean up.
