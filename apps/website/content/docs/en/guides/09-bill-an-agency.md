# Bill an agency and record its payment

For owners and finance. The agency has confirmed bookings on a credit contract.

1. **Check the agency's details.** Open any of its invoices, or create the first one (step 2), then **Billed to → Edit**: legal name, address and tax registration number as they should print.
2. **Prepare the invoice.** Open a booking, **Invoice**. To put several bookings on one invoice, choose them from the agency's bookings. Check the lines; add a note if needed.
3. **Issue it.** **Issue**, with the day's exchange rate to EGP and its source. The invoice gets its number and due date.
4. **Send it.** **Share** (phone) or **Download PDF**, in the agency's language.
5. **Record the transfer** when it arrives: **Payments → Receipts → Record receipt**, choose the agency, the amount, reference and slip. Its open invoices fill in oldest first; anything left stays as credit.
6. **Send a statement** at the end of the month: **Invoices → Statements**, the agency, the month, **PDF**.

If something was wrong on the invoice, issue a **credit note** for it; never a second invoice for the same booking.
