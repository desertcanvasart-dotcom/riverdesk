# Channels and contracts

A **channel** is anyone who sells your cabins:

| Type        | Example                                   |
| ----------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Back office | Your own office: phone, WhatsApp, walk-in |
| Agent       | A travel agency                           |
| Portal      | An online platform such as nilecrz        |
| API         | Another system connecting directly        |

Each channel has a **contract** per vessel saying:

- **which categories and rate plans** it may sell;
- its **allocation**: the shared pool, its own allotment, or on request;
- its **payment mode** (see [Payments](/docs/concepts/payments));
- its **markup or commission**;
- its **hold lengths** and, on request, its **answer deadline**;
- an optional **visible cap**, the most free cabins it is shown at once;
- **stop sell**, which pauses that one channel.

The contract is enforced on every call, in the office and through the API alike. A channel never sees or touches another channel's holds or bookings.
