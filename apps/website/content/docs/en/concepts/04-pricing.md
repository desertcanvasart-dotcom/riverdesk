# Pricing

## Rate plans and seasons

A **rate plan** is a price list for a market and currency, for example _International, USD_. **Seasons** are date ranges (High, Low, Holiday). A sailing is priced with the season of its embark date.

A plan prices either:

- **per person** (by occupancy: single, double, triple) or **per cabin**;
- **per night** or **per package** (the whole itinerary).

## A worked example

Two adults and a 7-year-old in a Standard cabin, LXR-4, per-person package plan, double rate USD 585:

| Line                            | Amount       |
| ------------------------------- | ------------ |
| Adult (double)                  | 585.00       |
| Adult (double)                  | 585.00       |
| Child 7 (50% of the adult rate) | 292.50       |
| **Total**                       | **1,462.50** |

Each line is rounded on its own, so the total always equals the sum of its lines. Money is stored as whole cents, never as decimals.

## Children

The child policy sets age bands, for example:

| Age on the embark date | Price                                               |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–1                    | Free                                                |
| 2–5                    | 25% of the adult rate, when sharing with two adults |
| 6–11                   | 50% of the adult rate, when sharing with two adults |
| 12 and over            | Adult                                               |

A child who is not sharing with two adults is priced as an adult. If a date of birth entered later shows a different age band, the booking is flagged on Today, not repriced.

## Supplements

Extra charges on certain dates, such as a gala dinner, per adult and per child.

## Channels

A channel's contract may add a **markup** (the channel's price is higher) or show a **commission** (information for the channel, not added to the total).

## Prices are frozen when sold

A booking keeps the prices and policies of the moment it was sold. Changing rates later affects new quotes only.
