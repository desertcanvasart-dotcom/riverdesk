# Payments, deposits and refunds

## Who collects the money

Each channel's contract says:

| Mode                 | Meaning                                                   | Booking is confirmed            |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **Channel collects** | The platform takes the guest's money and settles with you | At once                         |
| **Prepaid to us**    | The guest pays you directly (your office's bookings)      | When the deposit is paid        |
| **On credit**        | The agency pays you later, up to its credit limit         | At once, while within the limit |

## Deposit and balance

The payment policy sets them, for example a **25% deposit** and the **balance 30 days before embarkation**. A booking made less than 30 days before owes the balance straight away. Today lists deposits due and balances due within three days or overdue.

## Currencies

Each rate plan has its own currency, so each market can be sold in its own: USD, EUR, EGP, GBP, SAR, AED, QAR, CHF, CAD and AUD (two decimals), and KWD, BHD and OMR (three decimals). A booking keeps the currency of its plan; amounts are never converted. A payment in another currency is recorded with its exchange rate, and the booking is credited in its own currency.

## The ledger never changes

Every payment, refund and correction is a new line. Nothing is edited or deleted: a mistake is undone with a **reversal**, so the history shows exactly what happened and when.

## Refunds

After a cancellation, Riverdesk works out the **refund due**: what was paid, minus the penalty and any fees kept. The booking shows _refund due_ until the refund is recorded.
