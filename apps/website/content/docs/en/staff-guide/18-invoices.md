# Invoices and credit notes

**Invoices** (under Reservations) lists proformas, invoices and credit notes, newest first. Filter by type, status, or **Overdue only**. Owners and finance issue invoices and credit notes; reservations can prepare proformas.

![Invoices](/screens/{locale}/guide-invoices.jpg)

## Who is billed

- **Agencies on credit, and platforms that collect from the guest** (such as nilecrz) are billed for their bookings. One agency or platform has one billing party; several bookings can go on one invoice.
- **Direct guests**, and guests of an agency who pay the ship themselves, are billed in their own name, from the lead guest.

Edit a party's legal name, address and tax registration number from any of its invoices (**Billed to → Edit**). An issued invoice keeps the details it was issued with.

## From a booking to an invoice

On a booking, **Proforma** prepares a «فاتورة مبدئية» with no number (useful before the booking is confirmed), and **Invoice** prepares a draft invoice. Lines come from the booking's price as it was confirmed — one line per cabin, plus amendment fees, and for agencies on commission the gross price with the commission deducted as its own line. A cancelled booking is invoiced for its penalty and fees only.

A draft can be edited: its language, due date, note and extra lines (a transfer, a supplement). A proforma or draft can also be deleted.

## Issuing

![An issued invoice](/screens/{locale}/guide-invoice.jpg)

**Issue** gives the invoice its number (`INV-2026-00042`), today's date and its due date (the agency's credit terms, or the company's default). When the invoice is not in the company's base currency (EGP unless changed), type the **exchange rate**, its source and date: it is printed on the invoice with the total in EGP.

An issued invoice never changes — not even by the owner. Any deposit or payment the booking already had is applied to it straight away, and the invoice shows **Partly paid** or **Paid**.

## Credit notes

**Credit note** corrects an issued invoice: the whole of it, or chosen lines and amounts, with a reason. It gets its own number (`CN-2026-00007`) and the invoice's rate. A fully credited invoice becomes **Cancelled**, and the booking can then be invoiced again. If the invoice was already paid beyond what it still bills, the extra comes off it and becomes the party's credit (see [Receipts and credit](/docs/staff-guide/receipts-and-credit)).

## PDF and sharing

Choose **العربية**, **English** or **Both**, then **Download PDF** or **Share**. On a phone, Share opens the share sheet with the PDF itself (WhatsApp, e-mail); on a computer it downloads. **Copy link** copies the invoice's page, for colleagues who are signed in. Drafts print with a "Draft — not issued" band.

**Invoice register** on the Invoices page is an Excel file of every invoice and credit note issued in a period, for the accountant.

## Finance settings

Owners and finance set, under **Invoices → Finance settings**: the company's legal name in Arabic and English, address, tax and commercial registration numbers, bank details, the note printed on every invoice, numbering (prefix, first number, digits — locked once used), currencies, default payment terms, taxes (none by default; inclusive or added on top) and **account codes** for the accountant's journal (see [Statements and reports](/docs/staff-guide/statements-and-reports)).
