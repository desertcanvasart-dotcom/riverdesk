# Receipts and credit

Every payment is also a **receipt**: money received from (or paid back to) an agency, a platform or a guest. **Payments → Receipts** (owners and finance) lists them with what each has paid and what is **left**.

![Receipts](/screens/{locale}/guide-receipts.jpg)

## Two kinds of money

- **A booking's payment.** Recorded on the booking (as before: **Record payment**). It pays that booking's invoice as soon as there is one; what is left stays with the booking, for its next invoice or a refund.
- **An agency's remittance.** One transfer that covers several bookings or invoices, recorded here with **Record receipt**. What is not put on an invoice stays as the agency's **credit**.

## Recording an agency's remittance

![Record receipt](/screens/{locale}/guide-record-receipt.jpg)

1. **Record receipt**, then choose the **billed party** (search by name). Its current credit shows under the name.
2. Enter the **amount**, **currency**, **method**, date and the **reference** (the SWIFT or transfer number).
3. Attach a **photo or PDF** of the slip if you have one (up to 5 MB).
4. The party's open invoices in that currency fill in, oldest due first. Change the amounts if the agency said which invoices it is paying. What is left shows as **Left as credit**.

On an invoice, **Payments → Record receipt** opens the same form with that invoice first, and **Use credit** applies money the party already sent.

On each invoice, the money is spread over its bookings, earliest sailing first, so every booking's payment status stays right.

## A receipt's page

![A receipt](/screens/{locale}/guide-receipt.jpg)

- **Pay an invoice** puts what is left on another open invoice. For an invoice in another currency, type the rate.
- **Take off** removes the receipt from an invoice; the money becomes credit again.
- **Reverse** is for money that never arrived or was entered by mistake: it comes off every invoice, and an opposite receipt is added. Nothing is ever deleted.
- **Attach file** adds or replaces the slip.

## Refunds

- **A booking's money** is refunded from the booking (**Refund**), as before. If that money still pays an invoice, issue the credit note first.
- **An agency's credit** is paid back with **Pay back credit** on the Receipts page: at most its credit in that currency, optionally against a credit note.

## Methods

Bank transfer, cash, card, agency remittance, and **collected by the platform** (money a platform such as nilecrz took from the guest and settles with you).
