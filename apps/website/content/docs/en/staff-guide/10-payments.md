# Payments

![Payments](/screens/{locale}/desktop-payments.jpg)

**Payments** is the ledger of every payment, refund and correction, newest first. Filter it by date and method. Totals per currency and kind appear above the list.

Nothing in the ledger is ever edited or deleted. A mistake is corrected with a **reversal**, an opposite entry, so the history always shows what happened.

## Recording a payment (finance and owners)

**Record payment**, from here or from the booking:

1. Find the booking by its reference (opening it from the booking skips this).
2. Choose the **kind**: deposit, balance or payment.
3. Choose the **method**: bank transfer, cash, card at the office, agency remittance, collected by the channel, or online.
4. Enter the **amount** and **currency**. If the currency differs from the booking's, enter the **exchange rate** (1 EUR = ? USD, for example). The booking is credited in its own currency.
5. Enter the **reference** (receipt or transfer number) and the date received.

A direct booking waiting for payment becomes **confirmed** as soon as the payments reach its deposit. If its hold had already expired and the cabins were sold, the booking becomes **paid, cabin unavailable** and appears on Today. It is never confirmed without cabins.

## Refunds

**Refund** shows the amount still to refund, which Riverdesk works out from what was paid, minus the cancellation penalty and fees. Enter the amount, the method, the reference and the date.

## Reversing a mistake

**Reverse** on a ledger line adds the opposite entry, with a reason. Use it only for a payment entered by mistake. Money returned to a guest is a refund.

## Receipts

Every payment also writes a receipt, and an agency's single transfer for several bookings is recorded as a receipt of its own. See [Receipts and credit](/docs/staff-guide/receipts-and-credit). A payment that came from an agency's receipt is undone on the receipt, not reversed here.
