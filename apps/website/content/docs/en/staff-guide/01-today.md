# Today

![The Today screen](/screens/{locale}/desktop-today.jpg)

**Today** is the first screen you see after signing in. It shows only what needs a person, grouped and counted. When everything is done it says _All clear_.

## What appears here

| Group                                           | What it means                                                                                                                                                                     | What to do                                                                                |
| ----------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Exceptions**                                  | Something the system could not settle alone: a departure changed with bookings on it, a guest's age that does not match the price paid, a cabin taken out of service while booked | Open it, decide, and resolve it                                                           |
| **Paid but the cabin is unavailable**           | A guest paid after their hold ran out, and the cabins had been sold meanwhile                                                                                                     | Offer another cabin or date, or refund. Riverdesk never confirms a booking without cabins |
| **Requests waiting for an answer**              | Agency requests, with hours left before the deadline                                                                                                                              | Answer them on [Requests](/docs/staff-guide/requests)                                     |
| **Options and payment holds expiring**          | Cabins held today that will be released if not confirmed or paid                                                                                                                  | Chase the agency or guest                                                                 |
| **Deposits due**                                | Direct bookings waiting for their deposit                                                                                                                                         | Record the payment when it arrives                                                        |
| **Balances overdue or due within 3 days**       | Remaining balances by their due date                                                                                                                                              | Remind, then record the payment                                                           |
| **Guest details missing before the cut-off**    | Bookings within the cut-off with names or passports missing                                                                                                                       | Complete the guests on the booking                                                        |
| **Departures in 7 days with unassigned cabins** | Booked cabins that still have no cabin number                                                                                                                                     | Open it on [Guests & manifests](/docs/staff-guide/guests-and-manifests) and assign        |

Each line opens the booking or screen it concerns.

## Tips

- Clear Today twice a day: morning and early afternoon. Requests have deadlines.
- An item disappears by itself once it is handled, for example when the payment is recorded.
