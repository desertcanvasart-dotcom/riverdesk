# Schedule

![Schedule](/screens/{locale}/desktop-schedule.jpg)

**Schedule** lists every departure in a date window, with how many cabins are booked and held. Below it, owners set up each ship's **routes** and the **itineraries** sold on them, and the company's **ports**.

## Setting up a route (owners)

Do this once per ship, after [Ships & cabins](/docs/staff-guide/ships-and-cabins). Choose the ship at the top of the screen first.

1. **Ports.** Press **Add the usual Nile ports** (Luxor, Esna, Edfu, Kom Ombo, Aswan, Dendera, Abydos, Abu Simbel, Cairo) or **+ Add port** for another one. Ports are shared by all your ships.
2. **Route.** Press **+ Add route**. A route is the ship's repeating cycle of stops:
   - **Repeats every**: 7 days for a weekly route.
   - **First departure**: the date of the first cycle. Its weekday is the departure day, for example every Monday.
   - **Stops**: day 0 is the first port. Add the stops in order with their day, and optionally the arrival and departure times. The last stop is the same port on the last day, where the next cycle starts: Riverdesk fills it in for you.
   - Tick **Board / leave** where guests can start or end a cruise. Departures are sold between those stops; Riverdesk shows them under **Each cycle sails**, for example "Luxor → Aswan · 4 nights, Aswan → Luxor · 3 nights".
3. **Itineraries.** Under the route, press **+ Add itinerary** for each cruise you sell: choose where guests **board** and **leave**, and Riverdesk counts the nights and suggests a code and names (for example `LXR-4`, "4 nights from Luxor"). Choosing a stop in the **next cycle** makes a longer cruise, such as 7 nights from Aswan back to Aswan.
4. **Extend schedule** (at the bottom of the departures) creates 18 months of departures and sailings. The ship is then ready for [rates](/docs/staff-guide/rates).

### Changing a route later

- While a route has no itineraries and no departures, everything can be changed, and it can be deleted.
- After that, its stops are fixed, so that what was sold never changes underneath. You can still rename it and give it an **end date**: no new departures after that date. To sail a different route, end the old one and add a new one starting after it. Departures already created stay; cancel them below if they must not sail.
- An itinerary can be renamed or **paused** (no new sailings; those already created stay on sale). It can be deleted only while it has no sailings and no rates.
- A port on a running route cannot be retired.

## Changing departures (owners)

Tick departures, then choose:

- **Cancel departures**: the vessel will not sail.
- **Move dates**: shift by a number of days earlier or later.
- **Change port**: for example when low water or the Esna lock means the vessel starts or ends elsewhere (one departure at a time).
- **Dry dock**: no button ticked needed. Choose a date range when the vessel is out of service.

Every change works in two steps:

1. **Show impact**: Riverdesk works out exactly what would change, without changing anything. It shows how many departures and sailings are affected, which sailings would close, and the bookings, holds and allotments on them, with links.
2. **Apply change**: done. Affected bookings appear on **Today**, channels are told, and every touched sailing is updated.

Bookings are never moved or cancelled silently: the office decides for each one.

## Extend schedule

Riverdesk keeps departures 18 months ahead. **Extend schedule** creates any missing ones up to that horizon. It never changes or deletes existing departures.

## Changes made

The list at the bottom shows every past change with its dates and reason.
