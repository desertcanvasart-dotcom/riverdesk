# Requests

![Requests](/screens/{locale}/desktop-requests.jpg)

Some agencies book **on request**: they ask, and you decide. Their requests take no cabins until you accept.

Each request shows the agency, the sailing, the cabins and occupancy, the price, and **time left** before the deadline. The deadline is the agency's contract (6 hours by default) plus a short grace period. The most urgent requests come first. A request turns red in its last two hours.

## Your three answers

- **Accept**: the cabins are taken and the booking is confirmed. If the cabins are gone by now, Riverdesk says so and you can offer another date instead.
- **Offer another sailing**: choose a sailing with room. The agency has 48 hours (a company setting) to accept. The request shows _Alternative offered_ meanwhile.
- **Decline**: give a reason. The agency sees it.

The agency is told of your answer at once: in their system if they are connected, otherwise when they check.

If you do not answer in time, the request **expires** and the agency is told. Try never to let that happen: request response times appear on the Pilot dashboard.
