# Guests and manifests

![Guests and manifests](/screens/{locale}/desktop-guests.jpg)

## Departures

The list on the left shows each departure in the date window, with how many cabins are booked and how many still **have no cabin number**. Pick one to see who is aboard: guests of every booking on that departure, including those whose cruise started earlier or ends later.

## Cabin numbers

When a booking is made it reserves a **category** (for example Standard). The physical cabin (for example 101) is chosen later.

- **Automatically**: every night, departures inside the cut-off (7 days by default) get their cabins. A party keeps the same cabin for the whole cruise whenever possible. **Assign cabins now** does it at once for the departure you are looking at.
- **By hand**: **Change** next to a party lists the cabins that are free for its whole cruise. Pick one, or take the assignment off.

Two parties can never be given the same cabin on the same departure: the system refuses it.

## Cabin map

Switch from **List** to **Cabin map** above the guests to see the departure as the ship is laid out: deck by deck, in the order of the cabin numbers, each cabin with its number and category.

| Colour       | Means                                                                                                     |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| White        | Free                                                                                                      |
| Green-blue   | Guests: the lead guest's name, how many, and whether they boarded earlier or stay on after this departure |
| Red          | Blocked (hover for the reason)                                                                            |
| Grey, dashed | Out of service on this date                                                                               |

Above the decks, each category shows how many cabins are free, held and booked on this departure. Holds and agency allotments are counts, not cabins, so they have no place on the map until they become bookings.

**Moving guests** (owners and reservations): click a cabin with guests, or a party in the yellow **waiting for a cabin** box. The cabins that fit them on every night of their cruise light up in green, with how many there are. Click one: the party moves there for its whole cruise. **Take off this cabin** frees it (the next automatic assignment places the party again). Selling stays by category: the map only decides who sleeps where.

## Exports

| Document                        | Contains                                                                                              | Who can make it                                                                         |
| ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Manifest** (PDF or Excel)     | Every guest with cabin, category, nationality, date of birth, **passport number**, booking and cruise | Owners and reservations. Each download is recorded, and the file is deleted after a day |
| **Rooming list** (PDF or Excel) | Each cabin with its guests, adults, children, booking and agency. No passports                        | Owners, reservations and finance                                                        |

Choose the language next to the buttons. The file downloads when it is ready, usually in a few seconds.
