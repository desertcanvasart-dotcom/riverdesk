# New booking

![New booking](/screens/{locale}/desktop-book.jpg)

The booking screen is built for the phone call: you can confirm a booking in about a minute, on a desk or on a mobile phone.

## Step by step

1. **Booked for**: _Direct guest_ for a guest who calls or walks in, or an agency's name if you book for one. The agency's contract decides the price, how payment works and whether the booking is confirmed at once or sent as a request.
2. **Market**: who the price is for, from your list in [Settings](/docs/staff-guide/settings), such as _International_ or _Egyptian resident_. Only markets this channel sells appear. When a market needs proof, the screen says so and the voucher reminds the guest to bring residency or ID.
3. **Cabins**: the category, the number of adults, and the **ages of children on the embark date** (for example `4, 8`). Add a cabin for each extra room.
4. **Sailings**: pick the itinerary (for example _LXR-4_), then tap a sailing. Each sailing shows how many cabins of your categories are free. Sailings that cannot take your cabins are greyed out. Use **Earlier / Later** to move through the dates.
5. **Lead guest**: first and last name; nationality if you know it, picked from the list of countries (the usual ones first).
6. Check the **price lines and total**, then press **Confirm booking**.

You land on the new booking. Its reference (for example `VS-26-00610`) is what the guest quotes from then on.

## What happens next

| Booked for                                    | Result                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Direct guest                                  | The booking is **waiting for payment**. Its cabins are held for the payment policy's bank-transfer hold, 24 hours by default. Record the deposit on the booking and it becomes **confirmed**. If nothing is paid in time, the cabins are released |
| An agency that collects payment or has credit | **Confirmed** at once                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| An agency on request                          | Sent as a **request**: the button reads _Send request_, and the office answers it on Requests                                                                                                                                                     |

## Good to know

- Pressing _Confirm_ twice never makes two bookings.
- If two people book the last cabin at the same moment, one succeeds and the other sees _Not enough cabins left_.
- Children's prices follow the child policy: for example under 2 free, 2–5 at 25% and 6–11 at 50% when sharing with two adults. See [Pricing](/docs/concepts/pricing).
