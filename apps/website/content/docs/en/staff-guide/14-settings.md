# Settings

![Settings](/screens/{locale}/desktop-settings.jpg)

Everyone can read Settings. Only owners can change them.

## People

The list of everyone with a login: name, email, role, active or not. Owners can change roles, deactivate and reactivate people, see open invitations and revoke them, and invite someone new. See [People and roles](/docs/getting-started/people-and-roles).

## Markets

A **market** says who a price is for: _International_, _Egyptian resident_, and any you add, such as _Gulf (GCC)_ or _European_. Each rate plan is for one market and has its own currency, so a market can be sold in its own currency.

**+ Add market** (owners): names in English and Arabic, and a short code in lower case (such as `gcc`). Agencies and online platforms send this code when they book, so it cannot change later. Tick **Guests must show proof of residency or ID at boarding** for markets with resident or national prices: vouchers then say so, and **New booking** reminds staff.

**Edit** changes the names, the proof setting and the order, or stops offering the market for new rate plans (plans already in it keep selling). A market no rate plan or booking has used can be deleted.

Prices with three decimals (KWD, BHD, OMR) are typed with up to three decimals, for example `1.250`.

## Company

- **Company name** and **default language**.
- **Booking reference prefix**: the letters at the start of every booking reference. Fixed once bookings exist.
- **Defaults**: hold times, request deadlines, cabin assignment and allotment release days, passport retention. They are explained in [Setting up your company](/docs/getting-started/setting-up-a-company).
- **On every voucher**: the operations email (for the nightly outage pack), the emergency phone, and the voucher note in English and Arabic.

Every change is recorded with who made it.
