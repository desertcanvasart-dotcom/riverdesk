# Rates and policies

![Rates](/screens/{locale}/desktop-rates.jpg)

## Rate plans

A **rate plan** is a price list for one market and currency, for example _International (USD)_ or _Egyptian residents (EGP)_. Each plan says:

- **per person** or **per cabin**;
- **per night** or **per package** (the whole itinerary);
- its board, its child policy, its cancellation policy and its payment policy.

## Editing rates (owners)

1. Choose the **rate plan** and the **season**. Package plans also need the **itinerary**.
2. Type prices in the grid: one row per category. Per-person plans have a column for **single**, **double** and **triple** occupancy (the price per person when one, two or three adults share a cabin).
3. **Save rates**. The number of changes is shown before you save.

New prices apply to new quotes and bookings only. Existing bookings keep the price they were sold at.

## Supplements

Supplements are extra charges on dates inside a plan, such as a gala dinner at New Year: a price per adult and per child, mandatory or optional. **Add supplement** creates one for the chosen plan.

## Seasons (owners)

A **season** is a set of dates priced the same way, for each ship: for example _High_ (October to April) and _Low_ (May to September). Prices are per night, and each night of a cruise is priced by the season it falls in.

**+ Add season**: a short code (such as `HIGH`, it cannot change later), a name, and one or more date ranges. A season can have several ranges, such as Christmas and Easter, but a date belongs to one season only: overlapping dates are refused.

A yellow note lists the **sailing nights no season covers**. Those nights cannot be priced, so nothing on them can be sold until a season includes them. Extend your seasons whenever the schedule is extended.

**Edit** changes the name and dates. A season can be deleted only while it has no rates.

## Policies (owners)

Policies are shared by all your ships; each rate plan picks one of each. A policy used by a rate plan cannot be deleted. Each booking keeps a copy of the policies it was sold under, so changing a policy never changes an existing booking's terms: the change applies to new quotes only, and channels are told.

### Cancellation

Steps by days before embarkation, each with the share of the booking kept. For example:

| From    | Penalty |
| ------- | ------- |
| 60 days | 0%      |
| 30 days | 25%     |
| 0 days  | 100%    |

The step for **0 days** is required: it covers the last days and a no-show. **Amendment fees** (optional) are charged when a booking is changed: for a kind of change (date, category, guests per cabin, number of cabins, guest names, or any), from a number of days before embarkation, as a fixed amount or a percentage of the booking.

### Children

- **Adult from age**: guests this age or older pay as adults (usually 12).
- **Children per cabin**: the most children one cabin takes.
- **Age bands**: from age 0 up to the adult age, each band priced as **free**, a **percentage of the adult price**, a **fixed price**, or **included** (no charge, but counts as a guest). Type only the last age of each band; the next band starts after it. Tick **with two adults** when the price applies only when two adults share the cabin.

### Payment

- **Deposit %** taken at confirmation, and the **balance due** a number of days before embarkation. Bookings made later pay in full.
- **Bank transfer hold** (hours) and **card payment hold** (minutes): how long cabins wait for a payment.
- **Cancel unpaid bookings automatically** after a number of days past the balance date. When it is off, overdue bookings only appear on **Today**.
