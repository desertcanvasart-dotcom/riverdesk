# Signing in for the first time

## Your invitation

You cannot sign up to Riverdesk on your own: an owner of your company invites you by email, with a role. Sign in with **the same email address** the invitation was sent to. Riverdesk links your login to your company the first time you sign in. Invitations last 14 days; if yours has expired, ask the owner to invite you again.

One login belongs to one company. If you work for two cruise companies, you need two email addresses.

## The two-step code (owners and finance)

Owners and finance staff can see money and change important settings, so they also need a code from an authenticator app on their phone. Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and 1Password all work.

The first time:

1. Sign in with your email and password.
2. Riverdesk shows a QR code. In the authenticator app, tap **+** and scan it. If you cannot scan it, choose **Enter a setup key**, type the key shown under the QR code, and choose **Time based**.
3. Type the 6-digit code from the app into Riverdesk.

After that, every sign-in asks for the current code. The code changes every 30 seconds. If one is refused, wait for the next. Your phone's clock must be set automatically, because the codes depend on the time.

> If you lose your phone, ask another owner to help. Never share your code, and never send a screenshot of the QR code.

## Switching language

Use **العربية / English** at the top of any screen. Riverdesk remembers your choice on your profile.

## Signing out

**Sign out** is at the top of every screen. Sign out on shared computers.
