# Setting up your company

## What we prepare with you

A new cruise company is added to Riverdesk by the Riverdesk team. We create the company and invite its first owner. We then work with you to enter:

- **The vessel**: its cabins, each with a number and deck, grouped into categories such as Standard, Superior and Suites, with the most adults and guests each category takes.
- **The weekly route**: the stops in order with their days (for example Luxor, Esna, Edfu, Kom Ombo, Aswan), and the **itineraries** sold on it, such as 4 nights from Luxor or 3 nights from Aswan.
- **Seasons, rates and policies**: prices per season and category, child bands, cancellation penalties, and deposit and balance rules.
- **Channels**: each agency or platform that sells for you, with its contract.

Riverdesk then builds 18 months of departures from the route. From that moment the office can sell.

> Rates can be changed on screen at any time ([Rates](/docs/staff-guide/rates)). New routes, itineraries, seasons and policies are set up with the Riverdesk team for now.

## Settings to check before the first booking

Open **Settings** (owners only can change them):

| Setting                        | What it does                                                                       | Usual value               |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| Company name, default language | Shown on documents; the language new staff start in                                | Your trading name; Arabic |
| Checkout hold                  | How long a channel's cabins are held while its customer pays online                | 30 minutes                |
| Option hold                    | How long an agency's option is held before it expires                              | 72 hours                  |
| Alternative offer valid        | How long an agency has to accept another date you offered                          | 48 hours                  |
| Unpaid booking held            | How long a direct (prepaid) booking keeps its cabins while waiting for the deposit | 24 hours                  |
| Answer requests within         | Your promise to agencies on on-request contracts                                   | 6 hours                   |
| Grace after the deadline       | Extra time before an unanswered request expires                                    | 2 hours                   |
| Assign cabins                  | Days before departure when cabin numbers are assigned automatically                | 7 days                    |
| Allotment release              | Days before departure when an agency's unused allotment returns to sale            | 21 days                   |
| Keep passports                 | Days after the cruise before passport numbers are erased                           | 90 days                   |
| Emergency phone, voucher note  | Printed on every voucher: boarding point, check-in time, what to bring             | Your details              |
| Operations email               | Where the nightly outage pack is sent                                              | A shared office inbox     |

Changes apply to new holds, requests and contracts. Existing ones keep their times.
